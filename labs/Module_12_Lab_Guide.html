Advanced Architecting on AWS

Lab 12 — Budgets (with Cost Explorer demo)

Objective

Build a monthly cost Budget with an email alert in your own Sandbox account. Watch the instructor demo a tag-grouped Cost Explorer report from the management account. The Budget alarm is the piece that runs in production — every account should have one.

Time budget: 30 minutes

Throughout, replace <you> with your slug. Budgets and Cost Explorer are global services accessed via us-east-1 — the region selector top-right is irrelevant for this lab. The SCP allows them either way.

Pre-flight

  1. Sign in to Sandbox<N>. Role badge top-right reads AWSAdministratorAccess @ Sandbox<N>.
  2. Have your own email address ready for the alert recipient.
  3. Cost Explorer access: in many shared-Org setups member-account access to Cost Explorer is disabled by the management account. You will probably see "You don't have access" when you try Part A — that's expected. Note it and move on. The instructor demos the report from the management account in Part C; you focus on Part B (Budgets), which works in every Sandbox.

Part A — Try Cost Explorer (2 min)

  • Navigate: top-right account menu → Billing and Cost Management, then in the left nav click Cost Explorer.
  • One of three things happens:
    • "You don't have access" banner → expected in this Org. Move to Part B.
    • "Launch Cost Explorer" button → click it. Cost Explorer activates; data starts populating but historical data fills over ~24 hours, so the chart is empty today. Move to Part B.
    • A chart with bars → your account has data. Skim it; the instructor will still demo Part C from a management account with richer data.

Part B — Build the Budget (15 min)

This is the part that matters. It works regardless of Cost Explorer access.

1. Create the Budget (7 min)

  • Navigate: Billing and Cost Management → Budgets → Create a budget.
  • Budget setup: Customize (advanced) → Next.
  • Budget type: Cost budget — Recommended → Next.
  • Set your budget:
    • Budget name: archadv-<you>-budget
    • Period: Monthly
    • Budget renewal type: Recurring budget
    • Start month: this month (default).
    • Budgeting method: Fixed
    • Enter budgeted amount: 5 (i.e. $5/month — intentionally low so the alert can fire today).
    • Budget scope: All AWS services (default — do not add filters).
  • Next.

2. Configure the alert thresholds (5 min)

  • On the Configure alerts page, click Add an alert threshold. Fill exactly:
    • Threshold: 80
    • Of: % of budgeted amount
    • Trigger: Actual
    • Email recipients: your own email (you will receive the alert; comma-separate to add more).
  • Click Add an alert threshold again, fill:
    • Threshold: 100
    • Of: % of budgeted amount
    • Trigger: Forecasted
    • Email recipients: same email.
  • Next.

3. Attach actions: skip (3 min)

  • On Attach actions — optional, leave empty and click Next. (Budget Actions can stop EC2s or apply IAM-deny policies on breach; out of scope for this lab.)
  • On Review, verify:
    • Period: Monthly
    • Budgeted amount: $5.00
    • 2 alerts (Actual 80%, Forecasted 100%)
    • Recipient email matches yours
  • Click Create budget.

4. Confirm delivery (2 min)

  • Plain email recipients (the lab's path) do not require a confirmation click — Budgets sends directly via its own service. Do not search for a "Subscription Confirmation" email; one will not arrive.
  • The Budget appears in Budgets list with status OK. Current vs forecasted spend may both read $0.00 if Cost Explorer data hasn't populated yet — that doesn't disable the alert; once spend actually accrues, the alert fires.

To see the alert fire today, you can lower the budget to $0.50 in Edit budget — typical in-class Sandbox spend is $1–3 and the 80% alert (Actual ≥ $0.40) trips within a few hours.

Part C — Cost Explorer demo (10 min, instructor-led)

The instructor demos from a management account where Cost Explorer has data. Students watch and follow along by reading on the screen; nothing to build in your own Sandbox.

Demo steps the instructor walks through:

  1. Activate cost allocation tags. Billing and Cost Management → Cost allocation tags; locate Course and Owner in the User-defined tags list; Activate. Note: activation only affects costs incurred after activation.
  2. Build a tag-grouped report. Cost Explorer → Reports → Create new report; Date range: Last 7 days; Granularity: Daily; Group by: Tag → Course; Filter: Course = archadv; Save as archadv-spend-by-course.
  3. Group by service. Same report, change Group by to Service. The top three services in a typical archadv class day:
    • NAT Gateway — usually #1 ($0.045/hr/student × class hours; surprises everyone).
    • EC2 — the bastion and ASG instances.
    • Storage Gateway m5.xlarge — only spikes on the Module 4 day; expensive if left running.

The takeaway: the most effective cost optimization is destroying the resource. Lab cleanups exist for a reason.

Validation checklist

Cleanup

Leave the Budget in place. It's the one artifact from this lab that should outlive the class — keeps you covered against runaway spend in this Sandbox while it still exists.

Stretch goals (only if you finish early)

  • Read the AWS pricing page for one service and quote the per-hour cost: NAT Gateway, S3 Standard, m5.xlarge EC2, KMS API request, CloudFront request. Pick the one you'd never have guessed.
